E-Waste: authorised agencies and rates

      
    

    
Under the E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022, a bulk consumer may hand e-waste only to a producer, refurbisher or recycler registered on the CPCB EPR portal. Sell e-waste only to agencies on a state pollution control board list, and keep the invoice and handover record for your annual return.

      What moves the price
      
    
        
  • Mixed e-waste in Ludhiana was listed at ₹42.26/kg on 24 Oct 2026; pickup quotes usually run ₹5 to 7/kg below that baseline.
    • 
        
  • Recyclers also earn EPR certificates on what they process. The 2024 amendment ties the certificate price to a band of 30 to 100% of CPCB's environmental compensation, and ₹22/kg is the widely reported floor. Producers are contesting the band in the Delhi High Court, so certificate value, and therefore recycler bids, can shift.
    • 
        
  • Sorted boards, cables and casings fetch more than mixed whole units. Lead-acid and lithium batteries fall under the Battery Waste Management Rules, so quote them separately.
